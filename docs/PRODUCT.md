## Users

The primary audience is managers and owners of transport companies and fleet operators evaluating management software.

## Product Purpose

The Datatransp website explains the DataTransp ERP and helps prospective customers understand its modules before requesting a demonstration or contacting the commercial team.

## Positioning

The existing site presents DataTransp ERP as a management system for transport companies and fleet operators. Do not add competitor comparisons or unverified product claims.

## Operating Context

Visitors may review product modules, integrations, customer statements, company information, and published updates while evaluating whether to contact Datatransp.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing routes, navigation labels, content, legal notices, and supported product details.
- Keep form field names, order, consent, validation, submission behavior, and contact destinations unchanged.
- Present only confirmed modules and integrations. The user-provided reference confirms the Alertas and Combustíveis e Arla capabilities for this update; do not present illustrative statuses or sample values as live product data.
- Do not invent company metrics, customer endorsements, capabilities, or integration specifications.
- Preserve responsive navigation, keyboard access, and visible focus behavior.

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Datatransp name and existing logo.
- Preserve the current red and navy brand colors.
- The user approved refreshing layout and appearance while keeping the existing brand.

## Evidence on Hand

The current site contains the company and product copy, module and integration details, customer statements and authorized logos, company metrics, legal pages, contact channels, and brand assets. Retain their current wording and scope unless separately approved.

## Product Principles

- Help prospective customers understand the ERP and reach the commercial team.
- Keep the site's existing information architecture and operational behavior stable.
